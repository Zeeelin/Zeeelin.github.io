Description of the Project

The Last Voyage of the Picasso is a gamified VR welding experience developed by Spark-Smith Studio at Carnegie Mellon University's Entertainment Technology Center. Players take the role of a spaceship captain and repair their vessel through immersive welding tasks, with an AI companion providing guidance and feedback.

Role in the Project

Programmer and Technical Designer

I designed and implemented core VR interactions and the overall gameplay flow. I developed a Metaball-based dynamic mesh system for the welding interaction and prototyped an AI-guided NPC system using Whisper speech recognition and an LLM.

Application/Software Used: Unity, VR, Whisper, LLM tools, Perforce
