Description of the Project

Trapped deep inside a collapsed underwater cave, you are alone and running out of air. With your diving mask cracked and your equipment barely holding together, survival depends on how well you can distinguish your own direction and move through the darkness. Use your gauge to monitor oxygen and depth carefully. Moving faster or diving deeper consumes oxygen more quickly, so every decision matters. Look where you want to go, stay calm, and choose your path wisely. There is no clear route, only your instincts, your remaining air, and the hope of finding a way out before it’s too late. BREATHLESS is a 3D-frist person adventure simulation of cave diving. It is made by a team of 6 CMU ETC students in under 48 hours for the 2026 Global game Jam.
